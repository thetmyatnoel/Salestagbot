# DESIGN fewest-taps
EMPHASIS: FEWEST TAPS ON THE PHONE. The seller holds the customer's cloth in one hand: the cash sale stays scan + ✅ (apps-script/core_bot.js:366-373; docs/design-round-3/SPEC-offline-first.md:25), the whole-sale-by-bank case stays one more tap on the account's own button (core_bot.js:272-282), and every rare case (split payment, new category, new account, new fabric, new design) is exactly one tap further than the common case, with the typed number or name as the only input. Trade-offs decided by this emphasis: direct account buttons instead of a two-level 'bank transfer -> bank' dropdown; the split asks the account first and then the amount so the app's keypad pops open at once (app/app_ui.js:602-608); a split group gives up the later 👤/💳/🏦 rewrites (undo covers mistakes) rather than adding a confirm screen; options are added where they are needed and applied immediately, never through a settings menu mid-flow.

## SUMMARY
1. Selling does not change: scan the tag, tap ✅ ရောင်း ၁. That is a cash sale (core_bot.js:366-373).
2. Paid into a bank account: one more tap on that account's own button under the sale, e.g. 🏦 KBZ · ဦးမောင်. The shop's registered accounts ARE the buttons (up to 6, then a 🏦 … list); the same buttons sit under payments, deposits and expenses (core_bot.js:272-307, 1402-1409).
3. Part cash, part bank: tap 💵+🏦 ခွဲပေး, tap the account, type the bank amount (the keypad opens by itself in the app). Three taps plus the number, no customer needed. The ledger keeps the cash part on the sale row and the bank part as a payment row in the same group, exactly the shape a delivered order already uses (core_orders.js:285-325), so receipts, the 21:00 line, the Credits view and the monthly PDF are right without new columns.
4. Bank accounts are registered once by the owner: ☰ → 🏦 ဘဏ်အကောင့် → ➕, type the name (e.g. KBZ ဦးမောင်) and, if wanted, the number. Or on the laptop in the Sheet. Each account gets a short code that never changes; the name is what everyone sees; hiding an account keeps its history.
5. Expenses: pick the category from the grid as today (3 taps: category, amount, how paid). If the category is missing, the last button ➕ အသစ် asks for its name and the expense continues with it; from then on it is a button on every phone.
6. The same ➕ idea works in the order flow: a design code the bot does not know is created with one tap, a new fabric is added to the list where it is asked.
7. Lists are kept on the laptop: the Sheet tabs as today, or the ⚙️ စာရင်းများ screen in Telegram Desktop or the app in Chrome, where the owner renames, hides and reorders. Phones receive list changes at their next sync (apps-script/core_sync.js:752-758).
8. Measurements: one form page in the app with all fields and one ✅ သိမ်း, on a phone, a laptop (Chrome) or an iPad (Safari); the Telegram one-question chat and the photo of the paper sheet stay as fallbacks.
9. Voucher numbers stay what they are: an order's number continues the paper book's series per shop (core_config.js:41-44; gas_store.js:346-373); the shop name and address on the book are not needed (Settings shop_brand_S1 etc. may stay blank, core_orders.js:38-40).
10. Nothing in existing rows changes and no new row type is added, so the live bot keeps working; a split can be undone within 30 minutes (↩️) or voided by the owner; attach the customer before splitting if the sale should be on their card.

## RISKS
- A split group has two live rows and a PAYMENT attached, so rewriteCheck answers 'complex'/'locked' and the card loses 👤, 💳 မကျေသေး, the 🏦 re-mark and the note (core_ledger.js:408-423; core_bot.js:258-268): the only corrections are ↩️ within 30 minutes (undoGroup reverses all three rows, :371-401) or the owner's 🚫 ပယ်ဖျက် and a re-entry. Mitigation: attach the customer before splitting; the split screen text says so; a later multi-row customer attach is possible but not in this build.
- Deployment order: a phone build that emits the 'setting' or 'measurement' entry kind against the live v3 server is quarantined as BAD_KIND (core_sync.js:393); the category would then exist only locally and disappear at the next whole-map pull (app/app_store.js:401-405) while rows keep the key (label falls back to the key, core_ledger.js:180-185). Server first, then app_min_version (core_sync.js:329-330); setSettings keeps keys with pending outbox entries.
- Two devices adding the same label offline produce two keys with one name; the server appends both and logs an Errors row; the owner merges by hiding one on the laptop; reports show the name twice until then. Keys are random so rows never collide.
- Account buttons grow the card: 6 accounts = two extra rows under every sale; beyond 6 the rest hide behind 🏦 … (one tap further). The owner should hide the generic BANK key once real accounts exist, or sellers will keep tapping it.
- Voice: until INTENT_SCHEMA.method is freed from its enum (core_ai_prompt.js:81) a spoken 'AYA' lands on no account; and a spoken split ('၁ သောင်း ငွေသား ၂ သောင်း KBZ') cannot be represented in one intent, so voice splits stay two steps.
- The Dashboard's expense-by-category block is generated at writeDashboard time (gas_setup.js:273-281): without the setSetting hook a category added in-flow is missing there (not even counted as other) until setupSheet is re-run; per-account totals exist on the Dashboard only if the optional per-key rows are added, otherwise in the monthly PDF.
- In Telegram the after-sale card is edited in place: an account added later does not refresh older cards' keyboards; only the owner's last money group gets the one-tap button on the add reply (setLastGroup core_bot.js:1684).
- Hidden categories: labels must stay resolvable for old rows, hence the separate expense_categories_hidden key; forgetting to read it in categoryLabel would print raw keys like c7f3a in PDFs.
- The measurement form depends on the app's first deployment and the Phase 0 gates P1/P3 (app/README.md:172-181; POS-PROJECT-PLAN.md:759), on the derived measure_fields_json key and on the 'measurement' entry kind; until then a laptop measures through Telegram Desktop's chat (32 round trips at 2-5 s, design-B-tailoring.md:293-296). The app still does not know orders (ordersEnabled false, core_report.js:14-16), so a mistyped voucher number leaves the session unlinked but saved under the customer (latestMeasurement finds it, core_orders.js:545-555).
- A laptop or iPad enrolled by the sister is a second device per person with its own receipt letter and series (core_sync.js:284-299); SPEC v1 assumes one selling phone per shop (SPEC-offline-first.md:44); harmless as long as those devices do not sell, and each pushed row must carry her staffId (core_sync.js:343-357).
- Telegram callback budget: SXA|<group>|<KEY>|h is 55 bytes; a custom key longer than 12 bytes typed into the Sheet is still dropped and logged (core_ledger.js:108-112); the auto keys are 5 bytes.
- Half-paid typed amounts above the row's cash or 0 are re-asked (split_bad), not clamped silently; a double-sent typed number cannot commit twice because applyAwaitedNumber clears the state first (core_bot.js:613).
