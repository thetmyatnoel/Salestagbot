## Voucher

Header: Letterhead (printed): သိရိခေတ် / SILK & COTTON HOUSE logo; Facebook: သိရိခေတ် Thiri Khit - Silk & Cotton House; address အမှတ် ၁၄၄၊ စမ်းချောင်းလမ်း၊ စမ်းချောင်းမြို့နယ်၊ ရန်ကုန်မြို့; three phone numbers (09 7xx..., 09 5xx..., 09 4xx...); email winthikhine88@gmail.com; Customer Name (printed English label) — handwritten Burmese name, best reading 'မော်ဆိုလတ်' (uncertain); Address (printed English label) — NOT used for an address: holds the phone number 09455042134 followed by '⇒ Viber', i.e. the customer's Viber-reachable number; Voucher No — 2531 (pre-printed sequential number; the carbon copy underneath carries its own number); Date — 19/9/26 (handwritten, d/m/yy); A small handwritten word above 'Customer Name' on the pink band, unread (possibly a staff initial or branch tag)

Line items: Printed columns: No | Particular | Qty | Price | Amount. On the sample only No and Particular are used; Qty, Price and Amount are blank on all four lines. Each Particular is one garment written over two physical rows in the form '<garment/colour descriptor> ဆိုဒ် + <fabric/material> [design code] [note]': the first token describes the piece (colour or style, e.g. line 3 starts with a pink/'ပန်းရောင်'-like word), the recurring word after it reads like 'ဆိုဒ်' (size/style — uncertain), '+' joins the fabric or trim, then a catalog design code (TSL-0018 on line 2, TSL-18 on line 3 — same series written without zero padding, TSL-0025 on line 4; line 1 has no code), and an optional note on the second row (line 1 repeats the customer name; line 2 'အမှောင်...'-like colour/shade note; line 3 'Viber ပုံပို့ရန်' = 'send the photo via Viber' written in the Qty/Price area; line 4 a note in the Amount area, unread). A crossed-out fabric word on line 1 shows corrections are done by strike-through. Lines 2 and 4 carry a tick/check mark at the left margin, apparently marking pieces already done or confirmed.

Footer: Total — blank on sample; Advance — blank on sample (deposit paid at order time); Amount — blank on sample; = Total minus Advance, i.e. the balance still owed at pickup; Signature — blank

1) The voucher is being used as a work order / order ticket, not a priced receipt: four custom pieces listed, no quantities, no prices, no totals. The money side (Total / Advance / balance) is either filled later at pickup or kept elsewhere, so the digital order must allow price and advance to be blank at creation and set later. 2) The structure maps cleanly onto the existing credit-sale model in core_ledger: Total = order value, Advance = DEPOSIT/first PAYMENT, Amount = Owed; one voucher = one GroupID with several line items. 3) TSL-0018 / TSL-18 / TSL-0025 are catalog design numbers from a design book or photo album; the same design is written with and without zero padding, so the system should normalise codes (TSL-18 == TSL-0018) and ideally pick them from a list. 4) The 'Address' line is actually the Viber phone number; 'Viber ပုံပို့ရန်' and Form A's 'ပုံပို့ပေးမယ်' show design photos are exchanged over Viber, so a customer record needs phone + a flag/note 'reachable on Viber' and the order needs a slot to attach a design photo (sent to the bot on Telegram, or noted as 'photo via Viber'). 5) One voucher covers several garments while measurement sheets are per customer, so link: customer -> measurement set (dated) -> order (voucher no, order date, delivery date) -> line items. 6) The measurement form's 'အရေအတွက်' (quantity) corresponds to the number of voucher lines. 7) Voucher number is pre-printed and sequential; the plan (section 12.5) already keeps a bot receipt number R1-000231, so the paper voucher number should remain an optional reference field. 8) Dates are handwritten in two styles (19/9/26 on the voucher, 15.10.2026 and 30.10.26 on the sheet); the bot should present date buttons, not free text.

## Uncertainties

1) Form A free-area note '... 109': first word is cursive and only partly legible; it looks like 'ကိုယ်...' (body ...) and the number 109 is clear. Given body length 74 is already in the table, 109 is most likely a full dress/gown length; treat as 'note' until the family confirms. 2) Form B two-line note: the only clear part is the English 'No Bra'; the Burmese before it is probably about a built-in bust cup/padding, and the second line (something like 'စီးကျင်း ... ရဲ့ ပါ။ ကော်...') is unread — may concern lining, zip or collar. Ask the family. 3) Form B customer name: best reading 'ဇိုလာ (သဇင်)', low confidence. 4) Voucher customer name: best reading 'မော်ဆိုလတ်', low confidence; the same word is repeated on voucher line 1. 5) Voucher Particulars: the recurring word after the first descriptor on every line reads like 'ဆိုဒ်' but could be another word; the fabric/trim words (e.g. 'ကာလာဖျင်', 'ကစီမါ', 'ပန်းက...', 'သဘုလာ...') and the line-4 note in the Amount column are not reliably readable. Only the codes TSL-0018, TSL-18, TSL-0025, the phone 09455042134, 'Viber', 'Viber ပုံပို့ရန်', the voucher number 2531 and the date 19/9/26 are certain. 6) The small handwritten word on the pink band above 'Customer Name' is unread. 7) Units are not printed anywhere; values are consistent with centimetres on both forms (Form B's 17.5 shows decimals occur), so the bot should accept decimals and assume cm. 8) The meaning of the three slash-separated values in လက်ဝ and လက်အရှည် (upper/elbow/wrist vs short/elbow/full sleeve) is inferred from tailoring practice, not printed; confirm the order of the three numbers with the tailor before labelling the three sub-fields. 9) Whether 'ဝ' under the trousers heading means waist girth or hip girth for trousers should be confirmed. 10) Label unicode: printed labels were transcribed as standard Unicode; the paper may use Zawgyi-rendered fonts, so any OCR of these sheets should expect Zawgyi.
